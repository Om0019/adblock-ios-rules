[Adblock Plus 2.0]
! Title: Pop-ups, Pop-unders & Redirects
! Description: Blocks aggressive ads that open in new tabs, steal focus, or redirect the current page.
! Note: The pop-under/malvertising networks (magsrv, gmxes, exdynsrv, exosrv, opoxv,
! exacdn, pemsrv, happyleafmotion) are blocked at the domain level in general_ads.txt,
! which already covers their popups — no need to duplicate them here.

! --- Global Popup Blocking ---
! Blocks new tabs/windows opened by third-party scripts
