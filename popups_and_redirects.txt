[Adblock Plus 2.0]
! Title: Pop-ups, Pop-unders & Redirects
! Description: Blocks aggressive ads that open in new tabs, steal focus, or redirect the current page.
! Note: The pop-under/malvertising networks (magsrv, gmxes, exdynsrv, exosrv, opoxv,
! exacdn, pemsrv, happyleafmotion) are blocked at the domain level in general_ads.txt,
! which already covers their popups — no need to duplicate them here.
! Note: A blanket "*$popup,third-party" rule plus generic window.open-blocking
! scriptlets were removed from this list. They blocked *any* window.open call
! site-wide, which breaks legitimate popup flows such as "Sign in with Google"
! OAuth popups and image/thumbnail lightbox viewers. The domain-specific
! $popup rules below still cover known malvertising networks.
